How It Works

From sign-up to your first lesson in a few simple steps.

  1. Parents register. Create a parent account and log in securely.
  2. Add student profiles. Add one or more children (or yourself, for adults) under one account.
  3. Choose lessons. Pick a course and lesson package [CHECK: describe your real package/credit process].
  4. Book a time. Choose a lesson time that suits your time zone.
  5. Learn and follow progress. Lessons are private one-to-one, 25 minutes, taught in English. Parents can view learning materials, announcements and monthly progress reports.
