Privacy

Draft for your review. Please have it checked against the privacy rules that apply where you and your students live.

What we collect

When you send a trial request we receive the details you type into the form: contact name, email, the student's first name, age group, English level, course interest, time zone, preferred times and any message.

Parent portal

If you create a parent account we store your name, email address and the time you agreed to this page, plus your WeChat ID and phone number if you give them, and any student profiles you add (name or nickname, age, English level, preferred course, and optional goals, preferred schedule and notes). Only you and the teacher can see your account and your students.

Why

Only to reply to your request, arrange lessons and teach well. We do not sell your information.

Children

For children, a parent or guardian should submit the form. We keep information about children to the minimum needed for lessons.

Where it is stored

This website is hosted by Netlify, and form submissions are stored in Netlify Forms. Parent accounts and student profiles are stored in Supabase, in a database hosted in Singapore.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by contacting faithabrasaldo27@gmail.com.
